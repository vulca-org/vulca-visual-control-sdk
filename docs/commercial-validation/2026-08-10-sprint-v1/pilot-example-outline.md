# Example Pilot Packet Outline

Status: `FICTIONAL_VULCA_DEMONSTRATION`

Customer status: none

Human-confirmation status: not supplied by a customer

This outline shows what a reply-stage sample could contain. It must not be
presented as a customer case, deployment, paid engagement, or measured business
outcome.

## Fictional Task

A multi-brand creative team has one approved product brief and 12 candidate
visuals for paid social, ecommerce, and a local-market adaptation. The team
needs to decide what can advance, what must be revised, and which accepted
constraints should carry into a second batch.

## Example Review Rows

| Candidate | Requirement | Observed issue | Evidence status | Proposed action | Human owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | Preserve the approved product form | Product silhouette appears altered | visual observation only | revise | creative lead |
| C02 | Keep supplied claim wording unchanged | Generated text is unreadable | visual observation plus supplied brief | reject generated text layer | brand owner |
| C03 | Do not introduce unlicensed likenesses | A person is visible; identity and licence are unknown | insufficient evidence | restrict until rights route is confirmed | producer/legal owner |
| C04 | Adapt layout for vertical social | Key product remains inside safe area | visual observation | candidate for approval | channel owner |
| C05 | Match the approved colour family | Lighting shifts the product colour materially | visual observation; colour tolerance not yet supplied | request tolerance, then revise | brand owner |

## Example Human Decision Record

- `C04`: accepted for the social channel by the named channel owner.
- `C01`: revision requested; preserve silhouette and remove the extra product
  unit.
- `C02`: generated text rejected; supplied copy must be inserted by the
  production owner.
- `C03`: withheld; no use until likeness permission is confirmed.
- `C05`: unresolved; the brief lacks an approved tolerance or reference.

The record should keep assistant suggestions separate from confirmed human
decisions.

## Batch-Two Reuse Test

Only confirmed decisions move forward:

- preserve the approved product silhouette;
- never generate final campaign copy as image text;
- keep the product inside the vertical safe area;
- require a permission route for visible likenesses;
- do not invent a colour tolerance that the owner has not supplied.

The second batch is evaluated on:

- how many of these constraints were applied without re-explanation;
- which constraints still required human interpretation;
- whether review rounds or elapsed handoff time changed;
- whether any carried constraint was wrong for the new context.

## Public Handoff

First-touch email should not attach this packet. Share a short public or
synthetic version only after interest, with the status above visible. The
production website can be used to show the product surface, but its packaged
demo does not establish live generation or customer use.
