## Objective

Within a 21-day validation window, seek one qualified design partner for a
small creative-operations pilot. The sprint is intended to test a narrow
commercial hypothesis; it is not evidence of customer adoption, revenue,
model improvement, or continual learning.

The current BP v8 and production website are treated as frozen inputs. This
sprint should not trigger another deck or website redesign unless a real buyer
interaction exposes a specific blocking problem.

## Decision Gates

- After 10 named-person contacts: if there are no human replies, stop and
  inspect route, role, trigger, and offer before expanding the batch.
- After 5 discovery conversations: if fewer than 3 describe the same concrete
  workflow problem, reframe the ICP or wedge.
- After 1–2 pilots: if the second batch does not reduce repeated explanation,
  review rounds, or handoff time, reframe or kill the decision-memory promise.
- Do not invest in parameter updating or continual-learning infrastructure
  until strong prompt, evidence, retrieval, OCR, metadata, and tool baselines
  leave a stable update-specific residual.

## Evidence Taxonomy

Keep these statuses separate in every report:

- `prepared`: an internal artifact exists.
- `sent`: an outbound provider accepted the message.
- `delivered_unconfirmed`: no explicit bounce was observed; inbox placement is
  unknown.
- `auto_ack`: an automated response, not a human reply.
- `human_reply`: a relevant person responded.
- `design_partner`: a person agreed to a scoped co-design process; not
  necessarily a customer.
- `pilot`: a bounded workflow was actually run.
- `paid_customer`: payment is independently evidenced.
- `customer_outcome`: an agreed metric was measured and confirmed.
