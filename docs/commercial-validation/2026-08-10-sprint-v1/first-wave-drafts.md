## Message Contract

- approximately 70–100 English words before the signature;
- one named person, not a generic team salutation;
- one company-specific observation;
- one workflow hypothesis rather than a statement that the pain is proven;
- one five-day, no-integration pilot;
- one simple owner check or workflow-check CTA;
- a clear reply-based opt-out;
- no academic links in the signature;
- no customer, approval, compliance, performance, or continual-learning claim;
- no guessed email address;
- one follow-up maximum after 4–5 working days.
