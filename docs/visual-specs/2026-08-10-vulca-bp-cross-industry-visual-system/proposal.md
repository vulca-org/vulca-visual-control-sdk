# VULCA BP Cross-Industry Visual System

## Intent

Rebuild the six-page VULCA BP so the company reads as a horizontal enterprise
visual-intelligence company, not a beauty-content vendor. The current product
entry is an enterprise AI content production line that can serve multiple
industries and survive model replacement.

Beauty remains one bounded, fictional deep-demonstration case only. The primary
commercial visuals must demonstrate breadth across consumer products,
industrial manufacturing, and cultural-tourism or cross-border localization.

## Audience

Chinese-speaking enterprise decision-makers, AI ecosystem partners, and
early-stage investors. They should understand the product, see a real interface,
recognize several enterprise applications, and believe the company can expand
beyond a single content category.

## Deliverables

1. One industry-neutral 16:9 company cover visual.
2. One consumer-product campaign visual.
3. One industrial-manufacturing catalog visual.
4. One cultural-tourism / localization campaign visual.
5. One actual local VULCA Prompt Studio screenshot produced from source code.
6. One existing VULCA before-target-after proof sequence, labelled as a
   fictional demonstration rather than customer traction.

## Acceptance rubric

- No single industry dominates the company positioning or cover.
- Slides explicitly separate company, current product, first buyer, and demo.
- Three industry visuals have distinct subjects, business jobs, and composition.
- No fake customer name, result, metric, certification, UI, or logo is invented.
- Product UI comes from the local VULCA widget source, not a generated mockup.
- The beauty proof occupies one slide at most.
- Apple-like restraint remains dominant, Claude-like warmth remains secondary,
  and Kimi-like sharpness remains a small accent without copying trade dress.
