### Core Visual Thesis

The viewer should first see a launch-ready beauty campaign, then immediately
understand that the defensible product is not the generated picture. VULCA
preserves the enterprise brief, product constraints, semantic objects, revision
decisions, provider boundary, and adopted result while the rendering model can
change underneath it.

### Demonstration Task

A fictional emerging beauty brand must launch one lipstick line in China and
the United Kingdom within seven days. It provides a product reference, a brand
palette, a short brief, channel formats, and protected product attributes. The
demonstration produces a premium hero candidate, decomposes it into editable
semantic layers, changes only the selected environment or localization layer,
preserves product identity and non-target regions, and records the review and
adoption decision.

- Do not show a real beauty-brand logo, endorsement, partnership, customer claim, or fabricated commercial metric.

- Use public examples only as research context, never as customer proof.
