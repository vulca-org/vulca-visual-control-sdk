# VULCA BP Beauty Launch Dual-Evidence Visual Series

> Partial OpenSpec alignment: status enum + RFC 2119 keywords. Does not use ADDED/MODIFIED/REMOVED delta markers. Full alignment deferred until /visual-spec consumption validates need.

## Intent

Create one coherent, brand-neutral beauty-launch demonstration that makes VULCA
immediately understandable to enterprise buyers and early-stage investors. The
series must combine the desire and polish of a premium commercial campaign with
actual evidence that VULCA preserves enterprise constraints, semantic objects,
revision decisions, and adoption state while the rendering model can change.
The work is a VULCA demonstration, not a customer case.

## Audience

Chinese-speaking enterprise decision-makers, industrial partners, AI ecosystem
participants, and early-stage investors attending the 11 August AI Agent event.
They may understand generative AI but should not need prior knowledge of VULCA,
visual-model post-training, semantic decomposition, or cultural-evaluation
research.

## Physical form

Four digital 16:9 master assets at 2560 x 1440 pixels, delivered as high-quality
PNG files with editable source records where the workflow produces them. The
assets will be placed into slides 1, 3, 4, and 5 of the six-slide VULCA BP and
must remain legible when the deck is exported to PDF and viewed at normal
presentation distance.

## Market

The fictional enterprise task is a seven-day lipstick launch for China and the
United Kingdom. The BP itself is primarily Chinese, with only short English
product or workflow labels where unavoidable. Localization must be expressed
through market-relevant atmosphere, channel treatment, and controlled visual
variation rather than flags, landmarks, national colors, or token cultural
symbols.

## Budget & deadline

Event-sprint scope with a hard delivery deadline before 11 August 2026. Produce
four final master assets. Allow at most two candidate-generation rounds for each
key visual and one focused correction round for the chosen candidate. Prioritize
the hero and actual VULCA evidence sequence over optional decorative variants.

## Color constraints

Use Apple-like restraint as the dominant visual discipline: warm ivory space,
ink black structure, precise material rendering, and generous breathing room.
Add Claude-like warmth and editorial calm, then a smaller amount of Kimi-like
AI-product sharpness. Use VULCA cobalt blue for workflow/control evidence and
one lipstick-carmine accent for the campaign. Avoid rainbow AI gradients, neon
cyberpunk light, generic red-and-gold luxury, and excessive glassmorphism.

## Series plan

1. **Commercial hero / slide 1** - A launch-ready, brand-neutral lipstick
   campaign image that establishes company ambition and premium enterprise
   relevance. It is concept imagery and must leave clean space for deck copy.
2. **Enterprise task / slide 3** - A clear visual account of the fictional
   business input: product reference, brand palette, protected attributes,
   China/UK channels, seven-day deadline, and required deliverables. It should
   feel like a real operating brief rather than a technical architecture.
3. **Actual VULCA run / slide 4** - A large, source-safe evidence sequence from
   the real run: brief, protected constraints, provider boundary, candidate,
   selected revision task, review state, and adoption state. Reconstructed or
   mock product screens must not be presented as live evidence.
4. **Control proof / slide 5** - A large before / semantic layer / targeted
   revision / after sequence from the same source image. The stable product
   angle and protected product identity must make non-target preservation
   visually inspectable.

The four assets must share one product identity, one campaign world, one palette,
and one evidence chronology. They are not four unrelated illustrations.
